Save your resume as a PDF

Your browser’s print window will open next. To get a PDF file:

  1. Set Destination (or Printer) to Save as PDF.
  2. Keep margins on Default and scale at 100.
  3. Click Save and choose where to keep the file.

On a phone, tap Print, then pick Save as PDF or use the share button.
